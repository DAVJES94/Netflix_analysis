# Netflix Titles Dataset: Insights and Recommendations

## Insights

1. **Content Type:**
   - Netflix has a higher proportion of movies compared to TV shows, indicating a focus on movie production.
   - **Recommendation:** Consider balancing content by investing in more TV shows to cater to binge-watchers.

2. **Top Genres:**
   - Common genres include Drama, Comedy, and Action.
   - **Insight:** Drama dominates, but niche genres (e.g., Documentaries or Sci-Fi) could attract new subscribers.
   - **Recommendation:** Explore underrepresented genres to broaden Netflix’s appeal.

3. **Rating Distribution:**
   - Top ratings are **TV-MA, TV-14, and R**, suggesting a focus on mature audiences.
   - **Recommendation:** Invest in family-friendly content (e.g., PG, TV-Y7) to attract younger audiences and families.

4. **Release Year Trends:**
   - A significant increase in content release from **2015 onwards** reflects Netflix’s push into original content.
   - **Recommendation:** Continue focusing on original productions to maintain competitiveness.

5. **Country Insights:**
   - The U.S. dominates content production, followed by countries like India and the U.K.
   - **Recommendation:** Diversify content by investing in productions from other regions (e.g., South Korea, Africa) to cater to global audiences.

6. **Duration Trends:**
   - Movie durations cluster around 90-120 minutes. TV shows have more variable episode counts and lengths.
   - **Recommendation:** Consider experimenting with short-form content (e.g., 10-20 min episodes) for mobile-first users.

---

## Recommendations Summary

- **Content Variety:** Balance movies with more TV shows and niche genres to broaden appeal.
- **Global Reach:** Invest in region-specific content to attract global audiences.
- **Family-Friendly Focus:** Produce more PG and TV-Y7 content to cater to family and younger viewers.
- **Original Productions:** Continue the trend of creating Netflix Originals to maintain control over content and brand identity.
- **Short-Form Content:** Explore shorter-duration content for mobile and casual viewers.
